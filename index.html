Why you'll like it here

Designed for people,
not for engagement metrics.

Every part of Gola exists for one reason: to make talking to each other feel good again.

🔗

Your profile, one tap away

Share a simple link and friends land straight on your page. No usernames to spell out, no searching — just tap and you're there.

🛡️

Know where you stand

A small green, yellow, or red indicator on every profile shows an account's standing at a glance. Trust, made visible.

👀

Reports that go somewhere

Flag something and it gets a real review — warnings, temporary restrictions, and follow-ups when it counts. Filing false reports has consequences too.

✨

Updates without the app store

When a new build is ready, you'll find it in Settings. One tap to download, and you stay logged in — nothing lost, no hassle.

Built in the open

who actually use it.

Feedback isn't a form.

No ticket queues. No black holes. Just people talking to people — which is rather the point of the app.

Good questions

Everything you're wondering.

Yes — free, no asterisks. No ads, no subscriptions, no paywalled features. Even the fun stuff, like custom theme colors, costs nothing and always will.

Post photos and thoughts, join in on comments, share 24-hour stories, follow people you like, and make your profile yours with your own color. It's the familiar social app shape — minus the parts that drain you.

Every profile carries a safety status: green means good standing, yellow means caution, red means restricted. When someone files a report, a real review kicks in — warnings, temporary posting limits, and removal in serious cases. And filing false reports can cost you your own reporting privileges.

Open Settings and tap Check for updates. If there's a new version waiting, you'll get a download link right there. Install it over your current app — you stay logged in and nothing is lost.

We're Android-only while we're small, and that's deliberate — one platform done well beats two done halfway. iOS comes later, once the Android experience is exactly right.
